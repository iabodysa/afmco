# AFMCO 0.0.23

## Moved to Apex

- Moved employee transfer requests to Apex as transport requests, with their status; create new requests in Apex
- Opening an old transfer request in AFMCO takes you to the same request in Apex; accountants keep the AFMCO list for payment orders

## Changed

- Added a welcome on the portal for newly arrived employees
- Hid the staff portal pages from search engines
- Added the IBAN update, leave request, contact and job application forms to the portal list
