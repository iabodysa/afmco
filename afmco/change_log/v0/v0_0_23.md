# AFMCO 0.0.23

## Moved to Apex

- Employee Transfer Requests are now **Transport Requests** in Apex. All 1467 requests moved with their status. Create new requests in Apex: [apex.afmco.sa/desk/transport-request](https://apex.afmco.sa/desk/transport-request)
- Opening an Employee Transfer Request in AFMCO takes you to the same request in Apex. Accountants keep the AFMCO list for payment orders

## Changed

- The portal welcomes the new employees who arrived at King Khalid International Airport
- Every page of the staff portal asks search engines not to list it
