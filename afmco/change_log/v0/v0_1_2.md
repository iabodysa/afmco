# AFMCO 0.1.2

Payment Requisition gains the Supporting Pack and Account Bot buttons, the desk ships its own sidebars and dock, prints carry the Saudi Riyal sign and a centred DRAFT watermark, and Arabic wording follows the house terms.

- Payment Requisition: the Supporting Pack button attaches the requisition pack PDF (Payment Request Form with approval history, then the attachments) to the linked Journal Entry.
- Payment Requisition: the Account Bot button sends a paid requisition with no Journal Entry to the Accountant Bot, and the form shows the bot's progress to Accounts users.
- Payroll Entry: Verify Data records its verification errors for any user who may read the Payroll Entry.
- Employee Financial Summary shows each section by the reader's access, pages rows five at a time, and shows the end of service estimate.
- Employee form shows dues and creates opening Leave Allocations from dialogs; Show Changes and Show Compact View are gone.
- Employee Connections group End of Service Settlement and Vacation Allowance under Dues, and Visa, Dependent and Insurance under Residency & Family.
- HR Manager and Payroll User read and write the restricted Employee fields.
- End of Service Settlement writes the service duration in years, months and days to the Employee feedback.
- Download Employee Card works when the ID number is empty and loads the logo and photo from the current site.
- The desk ships the People and Payroll, My Tasks and Financial Operations sidebars, the curated HR, Payroll, Accounts and Setup sidebars, and the AFMCO dock.
- Number Cards Monthly Payment Requisition Count and Monthly EOS Payment Requisitions Count replace the Expense Request cards.
- Every print of a draft submittable document carries a diagonal DRAFT watermark in Arabic and English, centred on each printed page.
- Tax invoices, cash vouchers, Payment Advice, Petty Cash Custody Receipt, Disbursement Order and Payment Request Form print the Saudi Riyal sign; amounts in words keep SAR.
- AFMCO ZATCA Print Format ships for Sales Invoice; credit notes print the bilingual tax invoice with its ZATCA QR and returns print their minus sign.
- Samsa and Al Hokair Tax Invoices print landscape by default with a portrait Samsa variant; Al Hokair Tax Invoice formats are removed from Sales Invoice.
- Bilingual, Tawasol and Contracting Establishment Tax Invoices head the Day/Hour column before Qty; the Journal Entry print fits its Accounts columns to the page.
- Arabic: house wording for 254 framework strings, Idara spelled with hamza, Leave as الإجازات, Supplier Ledger Summary and Declare Lost in house terms, and the ten sidebar labels translated.
