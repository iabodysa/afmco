# AFMCO 0.0.19

AFMCO moves to the new platform version with a personal tasks page and a new login page

- Added a personal tasks page that shows what waits for your action, what others did on your documents, your unread notifications and what you submitted
- Refreshed the login page with the company colours and a clearer email hint in Arabic and English
- Added logos to the home screen tiles
- Fixed links in the employee, payroll and bank file screens and in report emails so they open the right page directly
- Moved the people, payroll and finance areas to the new platform version with no business data lost
- Updated the printed documents with clear English names and a shared company header
- Added pages to issue bank salary files and bulk bill payments
- Added charts and figures to the payment request dashboards
- Fixed workspace icons and colours that appeared blank
