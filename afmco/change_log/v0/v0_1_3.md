# AFMCO 0.1.3

Payment Requisition takes SADAD bill details and multiple beneficiaries, Journal Entry attaches its own bilingual print and dossier, Advance Leave Salary replaces Vacation Allowance, End of Service follows the Qiwa calculation, and the careers page opens at /careers

- Payment Requisition: SADAD Payment mode shows and requires SADAD Biller Code and SADAD Bill Number in place of Account No, and the Expense Request report shows them when Account No is empty.
- Payment Requisition: the Multiple Beneficiaries (per attachment) check hides Account No and Beneficiary and asks for the spreadsheet attachment; Payroll (Salary) ticks it by default.
- Payment Requisition: Account No, Beneficiary name, Amount and Amount in Words sit on one row, with Remark in its own section below.
- Payment Requisition and End of Service Settlement: the Verify attachments button runs the Accountant Bot reading of the attachments and shows it in the Attachments section; nothing reads attachments on its own.
- Paying a Payment Requisition marks its End of Service Settlement Paid.
- Journal Entry: the Expense Request field offers only Paid Payment Requisitions, and a second live Journal Entry on the same requisition saves with an orange warning.
- Journal Entry: submit attaches the bilingual Journal Entry Voucher print and a PDF dossier named after the entry, holding its own attachments and ending with an attachments index page.
- Journal Entry: the Supporting Pack copies Excel attachments locked against editing.
- Advance Leave Salary replaces Vacation Allowance, with its statement and undertaking prints; leave allowance rows follow each contract year and keep rows already Paid.
- End of Service Settlement: the award follows the Qiwa calculation in years, months and days, resignation after two to five years pays one third, and a Paid submit relieves the employee after the last working day.
- Employee: a monthly job puts an employee with no salary in three months on Hold and emails HR Managers the list; Hold is refused by HR and payroll forms like Inactive.
- Employee Financial Summary and the WPS Salary File page open only to HR Manager, Payroll User and Accounts Manager; the WPS page has a Copy Email Body button.
- Careers page at /careers lists open jobs with an apply form for visitors; /job and /jobs lead there, and the portal header starts with Open jobs and Apex operations system.
- Removed: Employee Data Update 2024, Gosi, Muqeem, Registration Details, application for employment, SADAD Group, SADAD Calculation, WPS File Metadata, Team Care, Iqama Renewal Management and iQama Renewal Payment Management; Iqama Renewal Fee Tracking stays.
- AFMCO fields drop the custom_ prefix, and the migrate moves their data to the new names.
- Payment Requisition and End of Service Settlement carry the production role permissions; Desk User replaces Request User.
- Petty Cash due date is the issue date plus 30 days, Saudi Bank is named by its bank code, and Delivery Trip no longer requires a vehicle.
- Tax invoices print stock quantity under Day/Hour and day/hour under Qty, and the bilingual tax invoice prints the seller address on one line.
- Arabic: one glossary wording per term, and no Arabic row without a caller.
