# Afmco 0.1.12

Iqama Renewal Tracking moves to Awaiting Payment or Rejected on save once operations records the renewal decision

- An Iqama Renewal Tracking in Awaiting Operations Approval with Renewal Preference Yes and a Renewal Duration moves to Awaiting Payment on every save, including the list Approve Renewal action, so Create Payment Request accepts it
- An Iqama Renewal Tracking in Awaiting Operations Approval with Renewal Preference NO and a Reason of Not Renew moves to Rejected on every save
