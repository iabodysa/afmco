# Afmco 0.1.16

Leave allowance rows follow the dates of an earlier settled clearance

- Get leave allowance gives a contract year already settled by an earlier Advance Leave Salary or End of Service Settlement that is Approved, Paid or paid through a Payment Requisition that clearance's own start and end dates, marked Paid, when both dates are within 7 days of the contract anniversary
- A contract year that shares 2 to 7 days with a settled period starts the day after it ends, or ends the day before it starts, with its amount reduced for the removed days and a note giving how many days were removed
