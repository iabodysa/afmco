# AFMCO 0.1.7

SADAD Payment Requisitions from Iqama screens carry no placeholder Account No, and Amount in Words sits under Amount

- Iqama Renewal Tracking and Iqama Control: the SADAD Payment Requisition they create is marked Multiple Beneficiaries and no longer stores the text 'SADAD numbers attached' as its Account No.
- Payment Requisition: Amount in Words is no longer a separate field on the form; the saved amount in words shows as the description under Amount and clears when Amount is edited until the next save.
