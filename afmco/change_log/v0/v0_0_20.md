# AFMCO 0.0.20

## Renamed

- **Payment Requisition** is the new official name of Expense Request Afmco. Request numbers are unchanged and still start with PR. Open: [/desk/payment-requisition](/desk/payment-requisition)
- **SADAD Batch** replaces SADAD Group V2, with **SADAD Batch Item** for its lines. New batches are numbered SADAD-B. Open: [/desk/sadad-bulk](/desk/sadad-bulk)
- **SADAD Group Item** replaces SADADGroup2 inside SADAD Group and SADAD Calculation
- **SADAD Setup Account** replaces SADAD Child Table 1 inside SADAD Setup

## Added

- Journal Entry shows its attachments and those of its linked Payment Requisition at the top of the form. A click previews images and PDFs on the same page. Open: [/desk/journal-entry](/desk/journal-entry)
- Sales Invoice line VAT adds up to the invoice VAT total
- Bank Reconciliation Statement by Period takes a From Date and a To Date. Open: [/desk/query-report/Bank Reconciliation Statement by Period](/desk/query-report/Bank%20Reconciliation%20Statement%20by%20Period)

## Fixed

- Iqama Control works on version 16. Open: [/desk/iqama-control](/desk/iqama-control)
- The employee advance balance shows on Payment Requisition and on the Employee form

## Removed

- PR v2, an unfinished attempt
