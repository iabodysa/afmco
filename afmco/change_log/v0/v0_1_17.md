# Afmco 0.1.17

The Account Bot drafts a preliminary Journal Entry from Document Upload and updates it once the requisition is Paid

- An accountant can send a submitted Payment Requisition in Document Upload or Paid state to the Account Bot
- A Journal Entry the Account Bot drafts early carries the Preliminary check, and pressing Account Bot again sends the requisition back to the bot so it updates that same draft
- Expense Request on a Journal Entry offers Payment Requisitions in Document Upload or Paid state
